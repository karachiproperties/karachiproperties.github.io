DHA City Karachi FAQs

Which DHA City Karachi sectors are in possession?

Current Karachi Properties classification lists Sector 2, 3A, 3B, Sector 5, 6A, 6B, Sector 7, 9A, 9B, 12A, 12B, 13A, 13B, 13C and Sector 14 as possession areas. Sector 3C, 3D and 3E are Non Possession.

Does Sector 1 have sub-sectors?

No. In this database Sector 1 is project-based and includes DHA Oasis Farmhouses, Askari Apartments, Hampstead Villas, Woodland Villas and Prime Villas.

Are the remaining residential sectors possession?

No. Areas not included in the current possession list are classified here as under development until the database is revised.
